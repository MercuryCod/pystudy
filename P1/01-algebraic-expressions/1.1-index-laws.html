Recap

  1. 1Same base only: multiply means add the indices, divide means subtract.
  2. 2A power of a power: multiply the indices. A power of a product: apply it to every factor, the number included.
  3. 3A sum over a single term: divide each term separately.
  4. 4There is no law for adding powers. \( x^{7} + x^{4} \) stays as it is.
