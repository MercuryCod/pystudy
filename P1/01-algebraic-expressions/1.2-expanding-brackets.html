Recap

  1. 1Every term in one bracket multiplies every term in the other. Count the products.
  2. 2Collect like terms only. \( x^{2} \), \( xy \) and \( x \) never combine.
  3. 3Three brackets: expand a pair, tidy, then multiply by the third.
  4. 4A squared bracket always has a middle term. A minus outside flips every sign inside.
