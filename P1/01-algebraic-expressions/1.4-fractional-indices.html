1.4Negative and fractional indices

A fraction in the power is a root. A minus in the power is a reciprocal. The four laws still apply.

Recap

  1. 1\( a^{\frac{n}{m}} \): the bottom is the root, the top is the power. Root first.
  2. 2\( a^{-m} = \dfrac{1}{a^{m}} \). The sign of the power says nothing about the sign of the answer.
  3. 3A power on a bracket applies to the coefficient too.
  4. 4Rewrite \( \sqrt{x} \) as \( x^{\frac{1}{2}} \) and \( \dfrac{1}{x} \) as \( x^{-1} \) before using the laws.
