Recap

  1. 1Same base: add, subtract or multiply the indices.
  2. 2Expand every term by every term; three brackets give a cubic.
  3. 3Factorise with the \( ac \) method, and keep going until every bracket is linear.
  4. 4Fraction in the power means a root; minus means a reciprocal.
  5. 5Pull square factors out of surds, then collect like surds.
  6. 6Rationalise by multiplying top and bottom by the conjugate.
